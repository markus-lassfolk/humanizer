# Scoring tuning report — 2026-05-09

Mode: whole documents; FPR@50 ceiling = 0.1.

Knob meaning (current code):
- `densityCoef` — slope on log2(density+1) in pattern score (default **13**).
- `densityCap` — max points the density term can contribute (default **65**).
- `breadthMult` / `breadthCap` — points per unique pattern hit, capped (default **2 / 20**).
- `categoryMult` / `categoryCap` — points per unique category, capped (default **3 / 15**).
- `patternWeight` — weight of pattern score vs uniformity (default **0.7**).
